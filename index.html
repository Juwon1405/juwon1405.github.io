01

about

I work in the seam between incident response and automation: building agents that read forensic artifacts, correlate them against MITRE ATT&CK, and propose response actions that a human can sign off on.

Day to day — detection engineering, host & network forensics, MCP tool design, vendor evaluation, and the unglamorous infrastructure work that makes any of it possible.

Working theory — most "AI for security" demos fail because they're prompt engineering with extra steps. The interesting problem is the architecture: tool boundaries, deterministic correlation, evidence chains, audit trails.
